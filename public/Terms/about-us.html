About AstroRekha

Digital Readings & AI-Generated Soulmate Sketches

AstroRekha, operated by Visionary Era, offers digital astrology and palmistry content, AI-generated soulmate sketches and self-discovery experiences.

What we offer

Our web application provides personalized palm readings, birth-chart interpretations, compatibility and other available self-discovery reports, plus AI chat services. The products available to buy are shown in the application.

Our Soulmate Sketch experience uses an automated chat to collect preferences and generate a personalized digital portrait with AI. Depending on the package, it may include a written reading, a color sketch and optional add-ons. It is not a live conversation with a human artist or a hand-drawn commission.

Our Indian and US Soulmate Sketch pages offer regional checkout options. US orders use Stripe card payments in USD. Indian orders use the payment methods and currency displayed at checkout.

How delivery works

Customers review a package and its total price before payment. After successful payment verification, reports are unlocked in the applicable account or delivered through the order’s stated digital delivery channel. Standalone soulmate sketches are delivered by email within the delivery window shown for the purchased package.

What these products mean

Our readings and sketches are for personal reflection. They do not identify a real future partner, guarantee an event or relationship, or replace medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. AI-generated content may be inaccurate.

Your information

We use the information needed to provide the selected service, such as palm images, birth details or sketch preferences. Our Privacy Policy explains our use of service providers, analytics and advertising tools.
